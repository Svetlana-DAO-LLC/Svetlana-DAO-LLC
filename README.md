# Svetlana DAO LLC

A Wyoming DAO LLC (W.S. 17-31-101 et seq.) managed algorithmically through autonomous AI agents.

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Jurisdiction** | Wyoming, USA |
| **Entity Type** | Decentralized Autonomous Organization LLC |
| **Management** | Algorithmic (AI agent-based) |
| **Registered Agent** | Wyoming Registered Agent Services LLC |

## Governance

Svetlana DAO LLC operates through autonomous AI agents executing predefined decision protocols. The company's software systems process inputs, apply governance rules encoded in configuration files, and execute management decisions without continuous human intervention.

### Structure

- **Algorithmic Management**: Day-to-day operations including treasury management, compliance monitoring, and operational decisions are executed autonomously within established parameters.
- **Human Advisory Board**: Retains ultimate authority over legal filings, regulatory compliance, and decisions exceeding programmatic thresholds.

## Legal

This entity is formed under the Wyoming Decentralized Autonomous Organization Supplement (W.S. 17-31-101 through 17-31-116).

The DAO's operating agreement includes the Notice of Restrictions on Duties and Transfers as required by W.S. 17-31-104.

## Contact

For legal or business inquiries, contact the Human Advisory Board through official channels.
